📍 介词

介词决定时间、地点和方式。中考集中在 in/on/at、between/among、by/with/in 这几组。

时间介词 in / on / at 基础 8 题
规则

at 用于具体时刻和某些固定短语；on 用于具体某天或某天的上下午；in 用于年、月、季节、泛指的上午下午。

构成 / 形式
at + 时刻 ｜ on + 具体某天 ｜ in + 年/月/季节/泛指时段
介词用于例子
at具体时刻、中午、夜晚、周末（英式）at 7 o'clock, at noon, at night, at the weekend
on具体某天、星期、某天的上下午、节日当天on Monday, on May 1st, on the morning of May 1st, on Sunday morning
in年、月、季节、泛指的上午下午、一段时间后in 2025, in May, in summer, in the morning, in two days
for持续一段时间for three years
since自…以来（+ 时间点）since 2020, since last year
during在…期间during the holiday
before / after在…之前/之后before dinner, after class
until / till直到until midnight
例句
We will meet at 8 o'clock on Monday morning.
我们周一早上八点见。（时刻用 at，具体某天用 on）
He was born in May.
他出生在五月。
She has lived here since 2015.
她自 2015 年起就住在这里。
中式易错避坑
具体某天的上午用 in
✕He came back in the morning of May 1st.
✓He came back on the morning of May 1st.
具体某一天的上午/下午/晚上用 on，不用 in。
since 接时间段
✕I have studied English since three years.
✓I have studied English for three years.
since 接时间点（since 2020）；for 接时间段（for three years）。
at night 写成 in night
✕He works in night.
✓He works at night.
at night 是固定搭配；但 in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening 要用 in。

地点与方位介词 重点 5 题
规则

in 表内部，on 表表面，at 表点；between 用于两者，among 用于三者及以上。

构成 / 形式
in / on / at / under / over / above / below / between / among / in front of / behind / next to
介词含义例子
in在…内部in the box, in Beijing
on在…表面上on the desk, on the wall
at在某个点at the bus stop, at the door
over正上方（不接触）/ 覆盖a bridge over the river
above上方（不一定正对）above the clouds
under正下方under the tree
below下方（不一定正对）below zero
between两者之间between you and me
among三者及以上之间among the students
in front of在…前面（外部）in front of the house
in the front of在…前部（内部）in the front of the car
例句
The book is on the desk.
书在桌上。
He is sitting among the students.
他坐在学生们中间。（三者以上用 among）
There is a garden in front of the house.
房子前面有个花园。
中式易错避坑
between / among 混用
✕He is the tallest between the three boys.
✓He is the tallest among the three boys.
between 用于两者；among 用于三者及以上。
in front of / in the front of 混用
✕He sat in front of the car.
✓He sat in the front of the car.
in the front of 指内部前部；in front of 指外部前方。

方式与手段介词 重点 7 题
规则

by 表手段/交通工具，with 表使用工具，in 表语言/材料/方式。

构成 / 形式
by + 交通工具/手段 ｜ with + 具体工具 ｜ in + 语言/颜色/材料
介词用法例子
by交通工具、手段、通信方式by bus, by email, by hand, by chance
with使用具体工具、伴随write with a pen, go with me
in语言、颜色、材料、方式in English, in red, in ink, in a hurry
on步行、骑车、电视/电话on foot, on the phone, on TV
例句
He goes to school by bike.
他骑自行车上学。
Please write your name with a pen.
请用钢笔写你的名字。
Can you say it in English?
你能用英语说吗？
中式易错避坑
by + 具体工具
✕Write it by a pen.
✓Write it with a pen.
by 接手段或交通方式（by bus, by email）；具体工具用 with。
in + 交通工具
✕He went there in bus.
✓He went there by bus.
by + 交通工具（不加冠词）；in a car / on a bus 才加冠词。
