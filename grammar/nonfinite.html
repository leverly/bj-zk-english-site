🧩 非谓语动词

一个句子里只能有一个谓语动词，其他动词要变成不定式、动名词或分词。

动词不定式 to do 重点 7 题
规则

不定式可作主语、宾语、定语、状语、表语。常见后接不定式的动词：want, hope, decide, plan, would like, try, learn, agree, refuse, promise。

构成 / 形式
to + 动词原形
用法例句
作宾语I want to go home.
作目的状语He went there to see his friend.
作定语（后置）I have something to tell you.
作主语To learn English well is not easy.
疑问词 + 不定式I don't know what to do.
省略 to（make/let/have/see/hear/watch）Let me go. / I saw him leave.
例句
I want to buy a new bike.
我想买辆新自行车。
He got up early to catch the first bus.
他早起为了赶头班车。（目的）
Let me help you.
让我帮你。（let 后省略 to）
中式易错避坑
不定式符号 to 遗漏
✕I want go home.
✓I want to go home.
want / hope / decide 等动词后接不定式，必须有 to。
make/let 后加 to
✕Let me to go.
✓Let me go.
make / let / have / see / hear / watch 后接不带 to 的不定式。

动名词 doing 重点 13 题
规则

动名词可作主语、宾语。常见后接动名词的动词：enjoy, finish, mind, practise, keep, suggest, avoid, miss, give up。介词后也用动名词。

构成 / 形式
V-ing
搭配例句
只接 doingenjoy doing, finish doing, mind doing, practise doing, keep doing, avoid doing, give up doing
介词 + doingbe good at doing, be interested in doing, look forward to doing, instead of doing
作主语Swimming is good for health.
既可 to do 又可 doing（意思不同）remember to do（记得去做）/ remember doing（记得做过）
例句
I enjoy listening to music.
我喜欢听音乐。
He is good at playing basketball.
他擅长打篮球。（at 是介词，后接 doing）
Swimming is my favourite sport.
游泳是我最喜欢的运动。
中式易错避坑
介词后用动词原形
✕He is good at play basketball.
✓He is good at playing basketball.
介词（at / in / of / about）后面必须接动名词 doing。
enjoy 后接 to do
✕I enjoy to read.
✓I enjoy reading.
enjoy / finish / mind / practise 等动词后只能接动名词。
look forward to 后接原形
✕I look forward to see you.
✓I look forward to seeing you.
look forward to 中的 to 是介词，后接动名词。

分词 难点 4 题
规则

现在分词 -ing 表主动、进行；过去分词 -ed 表被动、完成。可作定语、状语、补语。

构成 / 形式
doing（主动/进行）｜ done（被动/完成）
例句
The boy standing there is my brother.
站在那儿的男孩是我弟弟。（主动，正在站）
The book written by him is popular.
他写的那本书很受欢迎。（被动，被写）
I heard him singing in the next room.
我听见他在隔壁唱歌。（进行）
中式易错避坑
现在分词与过去分词混用
✕The film is interested.
✓The film is interesting.
interesting 表「令人感兴趣的」（物）；interested 表「感到感兴趣的」（人）。
see/hear + 分词与 + 原形混用
✕I saw him to cross the street.
✓I saw him cross the street.
see / hear / watch + 宾语 + do（全过程）或 + doing（正在进行），都不加 to。
