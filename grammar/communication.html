💬 情景交际

情景交际考的是得体。中考集中在问候介绍、请求建议、感谢道歉、应答告别四类。

问候与介绍 基础 4 题
规则

初次见面用 How do you do? / Nice to meet you.；熟人用 How are you?；介绍他人用 This is…

构成 / 形式
问候 → 回应 → 介绍 → 回应
场景问句应答
初次见面How do you do? / Nice to meet you.How do you do? / Nice to meet you, too.
熟人问候How are you?Fine, thank you. And you?
介绍他人This is my friend, Tom.Nice to meet you.
好久不见Long time no see.Yes, it's been a while.
例句
—How do you do? —How do you do?
——你好。——你好。（初次见面，双方都用同一句）
—Nice to meet you. —Nice to meet you, too.
——很高兴见到你。——我也很高兴。
中式易错避坑
初次见面回答 Fine, thank you
✕—How do you do? —Fine, thank you.
✓—How do you do? —How do you do?
How do you do? 是初次见面的问候，回答同样用 How do you do?；Fine, thank you 是回答 How are you? 的。

请求、建议与许可 重点 4 题
规则

Could you…? / Would you mind…? 表委婉请求；Shall we…? / Why not…? / How about…? 表建议。

构成 / 形式
请求：Could/Would you + V ｜ 建议：Shall we / Why not / How about + doing
功能句式应答
请求帮助Could you help me?Sure. / Of course. / I'd love to.
请求许可May I…? / Can I…?Yes, please. / Sorry, you can't.
提建议Shall we…? / Why not…? / How about doing…?Good idea. / That sounds great.
委婉拒绝Would you like…?I'd love to, but…
例句
—Could you please open the window? —Of course.
——你能开下窗吗？——当然。
—How about going swimming? —Good idea!
——去游泳怎么样？——好主意！
—Would you like some tea? —Yes, please.
——要茶吗？——好的，谢谢。
中式易错避坑
How about 后接动词原形
✕How about go swimming?
✓How about going swimming?
How about / What about 中的 about 是介词，后接动名词。
Would you like 回答用 Yes, I would
✕—Would you like tea? —Yes, I would.
✓—Would you like tea? —Yes, please.
Would you like…? 是邀请，肯定回答用 Yes, please.，不是 Yes, I would.

感谢、道歉与应答 基础 4 题
规则

感谢用 Thank you. / Thanks a lot.；道歉用 Sorry. / I'm sorry.；应答要地道。

构成 / 形式
感谢 → You're welcome. / Not at all. ｜ 道歉 → That's OK. / Never mind.
功能表达应答
感谢Thank you. / Thanks a lot. / Many thanks.You're welcome. / Not at all. / That's OK.
道歉Sorry. / I'm sorry. / I'm sorry to hear that.That's all right. / Never mind. / It doesn't matter.
安慰Don't worry. / Take it easy.Thank you.
祝贺Congratulations!Thank you.
例句
—Thank you for your help. —You're welcome.
——谢谢你的帮助。——不客气。
—I'm sorry I'm late. —Never mind.
——对不起我迟到了。——没关系。
中式易错避坑
感谢应答用 No, thanks
✕—Thank you. —No, thanks.
✓—Thank you. —You're welcome.
No, thanks 用于拒绝别人的提议；感谢的应答用 You're welcome. / Not at all.
