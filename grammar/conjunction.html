🔗 连词

连词把词、短语、句子连起来。中考重点是并列连词的搭配与从属连词的选择。

并列连词 基础 5 题
规则

and 表并列，but 表转折，or 表选择，so 表结果，for 表原因（书面）。

构成 / 形式
A and B ｜ A but B ｜ A or B ｜ A, so B
例句
He is young but very brave.
他年纪小但很勇敢。
Hurry up, or you will be late.
快点，否则你会迟到。
It was raining, so we stayed at home.
下雨了，所以我们待在家里。
中式易错避坑
because 与 so 连用
✕Because it was raining, so we stayed at home.
✓Because it was raining, we stayed at home.
because 和 so 不能同时出现在一个句子里。
although 与 but 连用
✕Although he is young, but he is brave.
✓Although he is young, he is brave.
although / though 与 but 不能连用。

从属连词 重点 6 题
规则

引导状语从句：时间（when, while, as, before, after, until, as soon as）、条件（if, unless）、原因（because, since, as）、让步（although, though, even if）、目的（so that）、结果（so…that, such…that）。

构成 / 形式
主句 + 从属连词 + 从句 ｜ 从句 + , + 主句
类型连词例句
时间when, while, as, before, after, until, as soon asI will call you when I arrive.
条件if, unlessIf it rains, we will stay at home.
原因because, since, asHe was late because he missed the bus.
让步although, though, even ifAlthough it was late, he kept working.
目的so that, in order thatSpeak louder so that all can hear.
结果so…that, such…thatHe was so tired that he fell asleep.
例句
I will call you as soon as I arrive.
我一到就给你打电话。（主将从现）
He was so tired that he fell asleep at once.
他太累了，立刻就睡着了。
Unless you hurry, you will miss the train.
除非你快点，否则会误车。
中式易错避坑
时间/条件从句用将来时
✕I will call you when I will arrive.
✓I will call you when I arrive.
when / if / as soon as / until 引导的时间或条件状语从句用一般现在时表将来（主将从现）。
so…that 与 such…that 混用
✕It was so a hot day that we stayed inside.
✓It was such a hot day that we stayed inside.
so + 形容词/副词 + that；such + a/an + 形容词 + 名词 + that。

关联连词 难点 4 题
规则

both…and（两者都，谓语复数）、either…or（要么…要么，就近原则）、neither…nor（既不…也不，就近原则）、not only…but also（不但…而且，就近原则）。

构成 / 形式
both A and B ｜ either A or B ｜ neither A nor B ｜ not only A but also B
关联连词含义主谓一致
both…and两者都谓语用复数
either…or要么…要么就近原则（看最近的主语）
neither…nor既不…也不就近原则
not only…but also不但…而且就近原则
例句
Both Tom and Jim are good at English.
汤姆和吉姆都擅长英语。（谓语用 are）
Neither he nor I am free today.
他和我今天都没空。（就近看 I，用 am）
Not only the students but also the teacher likes the film.
不仅学生，老师也喜欢这部电影。（就近看 teacher，用 likes）
中式易错避坑
both…and 谓语用单数
✕Both Tom and Jim is here.
✓Both Tom and Jim are here.
both…and 连接两个主语时，谓语用复数。
not only…but also 忽略就近原则
✕Not only the students but also the teacher like it.
✓Not only the students but also the teacher likes it.
not only…but also 遵循就近原则，谓语与最近的主语 teacher 一致。
