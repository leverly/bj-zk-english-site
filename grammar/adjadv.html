⚖️ 形容词与副词

形容词修饰名词，副词修饰动词、形容词或整句。比较等级是这一族的重心。

形容词的位置与用法 基础 4 题
规则

形容词作定语放名词前，作表语放系动词后（be, look, feel, sound, taste, smell, become, get, turn, seem）。

构成 / 形式
定语：a beautiful girl ｜ 表语：She looks happy.
例句
She has long black hair.
她有长长的黑发。（多个形容词顺序：长短→颜色）
The soup tastes delicious.
这汤尝起来很美味。（taste 是系动词）
Something strange happened last night.
昨晚发生了奇怪的事。（修饰 something 等不定代词时后置）
中式易错避坑
系动词后用副词
✕The music sounds beautifully.
✓The music sounds beautiful.
sound 是系动词，后面接形容词作表语，不接副词。
修饰不定代词时前置
✕He told me important something.
✓He told me something important.
形容词修饰 something / anything / nothing / everything 时必须后置。

副词的构成与位置 重点 5 题
规则

多数副词由形容词加 -ly 构成；但 friendly, lovely, lonely, likely 等以 -ly 结尾的词是形容词。

构成 / 形式
careful→carefully ｜ 特殊：hard→hard（努力地）, hardly（几乎不）, late→late（晚）, lately（最近）
形容词副词辨析
hardhard / hardlyhard 努力地；hardly 几乎不
latelate / latelylate 晚；lately 最近
nearnear / nearlynear 近；nearly 几乎
highhigh / highlyhigh 高；highly 高度地
friendly—friendly 是形容词，不能说 friendlily
goodwellgood 是形容词；well 是副词，也表「身体好」
例句
He works very hard.
他工作非常努力。
I can hardly hear you.
我几乎听不到你说话。
She sings well.
她唱得很好。
中式易错避坑
hardly 当「努力地」
✕He studies hardly every day.
✓He studies hard every day.
hardly 意思是「几乎不」，表否定；「努力地」是 hard。
good 当副词
✕He plays basketball very good.
✓He plays basketball very well.
修饰动词 play 要用副词 well，不能用形容词 good。
friendly 加 ly
✕He treats us friendlily.
✓He treats us in a friendly way.
friendly 本身是形容词，没有 friendlily 这个词。

比较级 重点 7 题
规则

两者比较用比较级 + than。规则变化加 -er 或前加 more；不规则变化要背。

构成 / 形式
A + 比较级 + than + B ｜ 变化：tall→taller ｜ beautiful→more beautiful ｜ good→better
变化规则例词
单音节 + ertall→taller, fast→faster, old→older
以 e 结尾 + rnice→nicer, large→larger
重读闭音节双写尾字母 + erbig→bigger, hot→hotter, thin→thinner
辅音 + y → ierhappy→happier, easy→easier, early→earlier
多音节前加 morebeautiful→more beautiful, careful→more careful
不规则good/well→better, bad/badly→worse, many/much→more, little→less, far→farther/further
例句
This book is more interesting than that one.
这本书比那本有趣。
He runs faster than me.
他跑得比我快。
The weather is getting warmer and warmer.
天气越来越暖和。
中式易错避坑
比较级前加 more 又加 er
✕more taller
✓taller
比较级只能有一种形式：taller 或 more beautiful，不能叠加。
比较对象不一致
✕My bike is faster than Tom.
✓My bike is faster than Tom's.
比较对象要同类：自行车和自行车比，不是和「汤姆」比。
比较级前漏 than
✕He is taller I.
✓He is taller than I.
两者比较必须用 than 引出比较对象。

最高级 重点 4 题
规则

三者及以上比较用最高级，前面加 the，后面常用 in / of 表示范围。

构成 / 形式
the + 最高级 + in/of ｜ tall→the tallest ｜ good→the best
变化规则例词
单音节 + esttall→the tallest, fast→the fastest
以 e 结尾 + stnice→the nicest, large→the largest
重读闭音节双写 + estbig→the biggest, hot→the hottest
辅音 + y → iesthappy→the happiest, early→the earliest
多音节前加 mostbeautiful→the most beautiful
不规则good→the best, bad→the worst, many/much→the most, little→the least, far→the farthest/furthest
例句
She is the best student in our class.
她是我们班最好的学生。（in + 范围）
This is the most interesting book of the three.
这是三本书里最有趣的。（of + 范围）
The Yellow River is the second longest river in China.
黄河是中国第二长河。
中式易错避坑
最高级前漏 the
✕He is tallest in the class.
✓He is the tallest in the class.
最高级前一般必须加 the。
in 与 of 用错
✕She is the best of our class.
✓She is the best in our class.
in + 地点/范围（班级、学校）；of + 同类人或物的群体（the three, us all）。

常见比较句式 难点 4 题
规则

as…as（同级比较）、not as…as（不如）、the more…the more（越…越…）、比较级 + and + 比较级（越来越）必须掌握。

构成 / 形式
as + 原级 + as ｜ not as/so + 原级 + as ｜ the + 比较级, the + 比较级 ｜ 比较级 + and + 比较级
句式含义例子
as + 原级 + as和…一样He is as tall as his father.
not as/so + 原级 + as不如…She is not as careful as her sister.
the + 比较级, the + 比较级越…越…The more you read, the more you know.
比较级 + and + 比较级越来越…It is getting colder and colder.
more and more + 原级越来越…（多音节）English is becoming more and more important.
one of the + 最高级 + 复数最…之一He is one of the best players.
例句
He is as tall as his brother.
他和他哥哥一样高。（as…as 中间用原级）
The more you practise, the better you will be.
你练得越多，就会越好。
Shanghai is one of the biggest cities in China.
上海是中国最大的城市之一。
中式易错避坑
as…as 中间用比较级
✕He is as taller as his brother.
✓He is as tall as his brother.
as…as 之间必须用形容词原级，不能用比较级。
one of the + 最高级 后用单数
✕He is one of the best player.
✓He is one of the best players.
one of the + 最高级 + 名词复数，表示「最…之一」。
the more…the more 结构残缺
✕More you read, more you know.
✓The more you read, the more you know.
「越…越…」两个分句都要有 the。
