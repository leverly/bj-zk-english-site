🔢 数词

基数词表数量，序数词表顺序。中考重点是百千的用法、分数表达和时刻读法。

基数词 基础 5 题
规则

hundred / thousand / million 前有具体数字时不加 s，也不接 of；表示「成百上千」这类概数时才加 s 并接 of。

构成 / 形式
two hundred students ｜ hundreds of students
情况写法例子
前有具体数字不加 s，不加 ofthree hundred books, five thousand people
表概数加 s，加 ofhundreds of, thousands of, millions of
两位数以上十位与个位间加连字符twenty-one, ninety-nine
百位与十位间加 and（英式）用 and 连接one hundred and twenty
例句
There are two hundred students in our grade.
我们年级有两百名学生。
Thousands of people visit the Great Wall every year.
每年有成千上万的人参观长城。
He is twenty-one years old.
他二十一岁。
中式易错避坑
具体数字后加 s
✕There are three hundreds students.
✓There are three hundred students.
前有具体数字时 hundred 用单数，也不接 of。
概数不加 s 或漏 of
✕Thousand of people came.
✓Thousands of people came.
表概数时必须用复数形式 + of。

序数词 重点 5 题
规则

序数词前一般加 the；拼写特殊变化要背：first, second, third, fifth, eighth, ninth, twelfth, twentieth。

构成 / 形式
一般 +th ｜ 特殊：1st first, 2nd second, 3rd third ｜ 整十 y→ieth
数字序数词注意
1, 2, 3first, second, third完全不规则
5, 8, 9, 12fifth, eighth, ninth, twelfthf→ve 加 th；eighth 少一个 t；ninth 去 e
20, 30, 40…twentieth, thirtieth, fortiethy 变 ie 再加 th
21, 22…twenty-first, twenty-second十位用基数词，个位用序数词
例句
Today is his ninth birthday.
今天是他九岁生日。
She lives on the twelfth floor.
她住在十二楼。
This is my first visit to Beijing.
这是我第一次来北京。
中式易错避坑
ninth 写成 nineth
✕the nineth lesson
✓the ninth lesson
nine 变序数词要去掉 e 再加 th。
eighth 写成 eightth
✕the eightth day
✓the eighth day
eight 变序数词只加 h，不是 th。
序数词前漏 the
✕He won first prize in the first time.
✓He won first prize for the first time.
序数词前通常加 the；但 first prize（一等奖）是固定搭配不加。

分数、小数与时刻 难点 5 题
规则

分数：分子用基数词、分母用序数词，分子大于 1 时分母加 s。时刻：整点用 o'clock，半点用 half past，其余用 to / past。

构成 / 形式
1/3 = one third ｜ 2/3 = two thirds ｜ 8:30 = half past eight ｜ 8:45 = a quarter to nine
表达英文说明
1/2a half / one half特殊
1/4a quarter / one fourth两种说法
1/3one third分子基数、分母序数
2/3two thirds分子 > 1，分母加 s
3/4three quarters / three fourths
8:00eight o'clock整点
8:15a quarter past eight过 15 分
8:30half past eight半点
8:45a quarter to nine差 15 分到 9 点
例句
Two thirds of the students are girls.
三分之二的学生是女生。
The meeting starts at half past nine.
会议九点半开始。
Three quarters of the earth is covered with water.
地球四分之三被水覆盖。
中式易错避坑
分子大于 1 分母不加 s
✕two third of the students
✓two thirds of the students
分子大于 1 时，分母的序数词要加 s。
分数作主语谓语数错
✕Two thirds of the water are polluted.
✓Two thirds of the water is polluted.
分数作主语时，谓语与 of 后面的名词一致；water 不可数用 is。
